Method & simplifications
  • Accumulation compounds each account yearly at the pre-retirement return; contributions land at year-end and grow by the contribution-growth rate. Contributions stop at retirement.
  • Withdrawals are pro-rata across buckets, so the tax mix of each retirement year matches your mix at retirement. "Spend to zero" is a level real annuity through your plan-through age; "4% rule" withdraws that percent of the balance each year (spending floats, money is unlikely to run out).
  • Taxes use 2026 federal brackets and standard deduction (Rev. Proc. 2025-32) applied in today's dollars — brackets are inflation-indexed, so this stays consistent. Pre-tax withdrawals are ordinary income; taxable-account gains use long-term capital-gains brackets stacked on ordinary income; Roth and HSA withdrawals are untaxed (HSA assumed for qualified medical costs).
  • The taxable account's gain share is fixed at its retirement-date value; dividends along the way, RMDs, Social Security, Medicare/IRMAA, and state bracket detail are not modeled. State tax is a flat rate you set.
  • All buckets grow at the same return. Single deterministic path — no market volatility or sequence-of-returns risk. This is an educational planning sketch, not financial or tax advice.
